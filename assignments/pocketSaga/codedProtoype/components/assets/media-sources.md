# Current catalog artwork

Local artwork is used by the carousel, reflections, page backgrounds, groups, title picker, and post attachments.

- `from-season-1.jpg`: From Season 1, teal promotional poster. [TVmaze asset](https://static.tvmaze.com/uploads/images/original_untouched/403/1009473.jpg).
- `silicon-valley-color.jpg`: Silicon Valley series artwork, colorful illustrated city. [TVmaze asset](https://static.tvmaze.com/uploads/images/original_untouched/0/1985.jpg). The catalog discussion scope is Season 1; this is series artwork.
- `dark-artwork.png`: User-provided extended Dark series poster with the Netflix logo removed. Replaces the [IMDb artwork](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt5753856/mediaviewer/rm2489953025/) previously used in the prototype. `dark-artwork-original.png` preserves the earlier committed poster for reference.
- Unused archive: `everything-everywhere-color.jpg` — Everything Everywhere All at Once was removed from the active catalog; multicolored theatrical poster. [TMDB asset](https://image.tmdb.org/t/p/w780/w3LxiVYdWWRvEVdn5RYq6jIqkb1.jpg).
- Interstellar retains its existing local artwork.

Catalog content is original fictional discussion text for the prototype. Season-level discussions use Season 1 for the relevant series. Earlier Whiplash and Prestige assets remain available but are not used by the current catalog.

- `coherence.jpg`: Coherence (2013), theatrical poster. [Source](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/9/9d/Coherence_2013_theatrical_poster.jpg).

- `inception.jpg`: Inception (2010) poster, copied unchanged from `../movieShowAsset/Inception (2010).jpg` (IMDb Top Rated collection). Used only by the Home screen's "Your next movie night" suggestions, together with the archived `prestige.jpg` and `whiplash.jpg`.
